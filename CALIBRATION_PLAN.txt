HISTORICAL CALIBRATION PLAN

The first dataset is an EVENT-STUDY dataset, not yet the final prediction dataset.

Why:
- Official Fed pages provide reliable FOMC dates/statements/SEPs.
- FRED provides daily DGS2, DGS10 and DFII10 Treasury series.
- Market prices can provide the reaction label.
- Historical consensus forecasts are a separate data problem and should not be
  invented.

Official FOMC calendars list meetings and identify SEP meetings. The Fed also
publishes statements, minutes and projection materials. See:
https://www.federalreserve.gov/monetarypolicy/fomccalendars.htm

FRED's DGS2, DGS10 and DFII10 series are daily Treasury/yield series.

NEXT DATA UPGRADE:
Add historical consensus and actual values for CPI, PCE, NFP and FOMC/rate
expectations. Then compute true standardized surprises and run walk-forward
calibration.

DO NOT train and evaluate on the same period as a final performance claim.
Use chronological train/validation/test splits.
